TOKEN RADAR

Pre-token protocols, ranked by what a token would be worth if the protocol’s record stayed as it is. A launch is never promised: the board states a value and a farm plan, not a date.

How the radar works

Implied value = TVL × 0.35 × category multiple × growth × maturity × chains. Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −40%…+60%. Maturity rises with the days since DefiLlama first listed the protocol, up to three years. Chains counts how many chains the protocol runs on, up to twelve. Category multiples: Prediction Market 1.4, RWA 1.2, Risk Curators, Onchain Capital Allocator and Payments 1.1, Yield and Basis Trading 1.0, Dexs, Collateral Markets, Perpetuals and Derivatives 0.9, Lending and Insurance 0.8, CDP and Stablecoin 0.7, everything else 0.85. The same function scores every protocol; no name is special-cased.

A protocol “records no token” when DefiLlama carries no market cap, CoinGecko id or symbol for it. That is the record, not proof it has none. The watchlist is the floor: 40 protocols at or above $50M of TVL that pass the house rule. The board adds 158 more pre-token protocols below the floor that pass the same rule, so the radar finds launches early instead of only listing the big ones.
